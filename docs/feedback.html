Beta testing

Help make MicroBoy better

Thanks for testing. Every bug report, rough edge and idea helps. Try as many features as you can, on as many games as you own, and tell us what worked, what didn't, and what felt confusing.

What to try

Tick things off as you go. Your progress is saved in this browser only.

What feedback we want

  • Bugs: crashes, freezes, wrong graphics or sound, lost saves, buttons that don't respond.
  • Performance: stutter, slowdowns, battery drain or heat. Mention your phone model.
  • Online link: did linking work? The Link cable screen shows "direct" or "via relay" and the input delay. Tell us which, and how it felt.
  • Confusing parts: anything you had to figure out, or couldn't find.
  • Ideas: features you'd love to see.
  • Things you liked: so we know what not to break.

A great bug report

  1. What you did, step by step.
  2. What happened, and what you expected.
  3. The game, and whether it happens every time.
  4. Your phone and Android version. In-app feedback fills these in for you.
  5. A screenshot or screen recording, if you can. Attach it to the email.

Other ways to reach us

In the app

Settings → About → Send feedback, or the feedback button on the library screen. It fills in your MicroBoy version and phone details for you.

Google Play (private)

Testers can send private feedback through Google Play: open MicroBoy's page in the Play Store and use the tester feedback section. Only the developer sees it.
