## Full description (4000 max)

MicroBoy is a fast, accurate GBA emulator for Android, built on the respected mGBA core. Play your game backups with real fast-forward, rewind, save states and an online link cable for trading and battling with friends.

ONLINE LINK CABLE
• Trade and battle over Wi-Fi or the internet, like a link cable that reaches anywhere.
• Both phones run both games and only send button presses, so linked play runs at full speed.
• Find players in the lobby: see what people are playing, or connect by nickname and pick a game together.
• Phones connect directly when they can, and through a relay when they can't.
• Block or report any player. Nicknames are filtered.

SPEED CONTROLS
• Real fast-forward at 2×, 3×, 4× or 8×, or as fast as your phone allows.
• Slow motion at ¾×, ½× or ¼×, with the sound slowed to match.
• Rewind: hold ◀◀ to go back up to a minute. Rewinding never undoes your in-game save.

SAVES YOU CAN TRUST
• Your in-game saves are kept separate from save states and are never overwritten by them.
• Nine save-state slots plus Auto, with thumbnails, quick save and load, and export and import.
• MicroBoy asks before overwriting anything.
• Import and export standard .sav files.

CONTROLS
• Multi-touch on-screen controls with haptics. Move and resize every button, in portrait and landscape.
• Bluetooth and USB controllers, with remappable buttons.
• Cartridge extras use your phone's sensors: tilt, gyro, solar sensor and rumble.

DISPLAY AND SOUND
• Fit, integer or stretch scaling, smoothing, and scanline, LCD-grid and CRT filters.
• Brightness, saturation and GBA color correction.
• Low-latency audio that stays in sync, and pauses when you unplug your headphones.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Eleven color themes, a fully custom theme, and your wallpaper colors on Android 12+.
• Your library keeps games where they are on your phone, with recently played, playtime and covers.

ONE PRICE, NO ADS
• No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no accounts.
• Free demo ROM, with its own song, on our website.

IMPORTANT
• MicroBoy does not include any games or BIOS files. Play backups of games you own.
• Online link requires both players to have the games involved. Game files are never shared.
