Privacy policy

MicroBoy has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash-reporting services. Everything stays on your device unless you use one of the optional features below: online link, box-art download, or sending feedback.

What stays on your device

Your games, in-game saves, save states, save-state thumbnails, playtime and settings stay on your device. MicroBoy reads your games where you keep them, through Android's file picker, and only touches files and folders you choose.

Optional box-art download

MicroBoy can download cover art for your library. This is off by default and only runs if you turn on Download box art in Settings.

When it's on, MicroBoy requests an image from the libretro thumbnail database (thumbnails.libretro.com). The request contains only the game's file name, which is used to find a matching cover. Like any web request, it also shows the libretro server your IP address. MicroBoy sends nothing else, such as your game files, saves or device details. The libretro project runs that server under its own policies.

Online link (optional)

Online link only runs when you turn it on in the Link cable screen. While it's on, your phone connects to MicroBoy's link server, which runs on Cloudflare at microboy-link.masato-void.workers.dev:

  • Your nickname is shown to other online players. If you choose Show my game, so is the title of the game you're playing. You can hide yourself from the lobby at any time.
  • Player id. Your phone creates a random id when you first use online link. The server only ever sees a one-way, salted hash of it, which identifies you in the lobby so blocks and reports work. It can't be linked to your name, account or device.
  • Who's online is held in memory only, while you're connected. The server doesn't keep a history of players or matches.
  • When you link with someone, your phones exchange each console's save state (including the in-game save), the game's fingerprint (a SHA-1 hash) and title, and your button presses. These go only to the player you linked with. The server relays them without storing them, and the other phone keeps them in memory only for the session. Game files themselves are never sent.
  • IP addresses. Like any internet service, the server sees your IP address. To connect phones directly, the two linked phones also learn each other's IP addresses, and your phone asks Cloudflare's public STUN service (stun.cloudflare.com) for its public address. If a direct connection isn't possible, data goes through the server instead.
  • Reports. If you report a player, the server stores their player id, their nickname at the time, the reason you picked, the time, and your player id. Reports are deleted after 30 days. A player reported by three different people is hidden from the lobby for a week.
  • Blocks are stored on your phone, and sent to the server while you're online so the two of you don't see each other.

Homebrew downloads

When you open Add games → MicroBoy homebrew, the app downloads the list of free MicroBoy games, their covers and any game you choose from the MicroBoy website, which GitHub Pages hosts. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any website. Nothing else is sent.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app or the website, it goes by email from your own email app to masato-void@protonmail.com. We receive your email address and whatever you write, plus your MicroBoy version and phone model, which the app fills in and you can delete before sending. It's used only to fix problems, improve MicroBoy and reply to you. Feedback sent through Google Play is handled by Google under its policies.

Android backup

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include MicroBoy's saves, save states and settings in your device backup, which Google stores and encrypts for you. Game files copied into the app are excluded. This is controlled by your device settings, not by MicroBoy, and the developer has no access to it.

Permissions

  • Internet: used only for online link, homebrew downloads and the optional box-art download.
  • Vibrate: haptic feedback on the touch controls.

Purchases

If you buy MicroBoy on Google Play, Google handles the payment under the Google Privacy Policy. The developer receives only the standard, anonymized sales reports that Google Play provides.

Children

MicroBoy isn't directed at children. Online link shows only a chosen nickname. Nicknames are limited to letters, numbers, spaces and . _ -, so they can't hold links or contact details, and offensive names are rejected. Any player can be blocked or reported.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
